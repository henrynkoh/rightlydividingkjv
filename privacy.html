Effective Date: July 24, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Rightly Dividing KJV Publisher" application ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal automation script used solely by its owner (Henry Oh, henrynkoh@gmail.com) to publish original Bible study content to YouTube (@ohhenry6524) and Blogger (rightlydividingkjv.blogspot.com).

The App is not a public-facing application. It does not collect, store, or process data from any third-party users.

2. Data We Access

The App accesses the following Google services on behalf of its owner only:

  • YouTube Data API v3 – to upload original video content to the owner's YouTube channel (@ohhenry6524)
  • Blogger API v3 – to publish original blog posts to the owner's Blogger blog (rightlydividingkjv.blogspot.com)

No viewer data, subscriber data, analytics, or third-party user information is accessed or stored.

3. Data Storage

The App stores only:

  • OAuth 2.0 credentials (token.json) on the owner's local machine
  • A local tracking file (tracking.json) recording which posts have been published

No data is transmitted to any third party other than Google's YouTube and Blogger APIs.

4. YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using this App, you (the owner) agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions.

5. Data Deletion

To revoke all access and delete stored credentials:

  1. Delete the token.json file from your local machine
  2. Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove the app's access

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

This policy may be updated periodically. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
