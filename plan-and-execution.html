Plan and execution

One work, placed on the channel and on the three reading places.

Plan

  1. Gather the questions, doubts, criticisms, and suggestions people raise about church life and a religion-centered life.
  2. Answer each one with the King James Version alone, so the words of God are the answer.
  3. Publish that work on the YouTube channel, the blog, the study hub, and the master hub, and give every question its own address.

Execution

YouTube

Church life, answered in the KJV is on Rightly Dividing the Word of Truth. It states the plan and reads 2 Timothy 2:15 and John 3:16, then points to the three reading places.
